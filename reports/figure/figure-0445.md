## 4. Constructive mechanism and an exact own-centroid reserve certificate

For an escaping physical edge u→v measured about a fixed centre, put ru=|u|, rv=|v| and phi=angle(u,v). The positive arrow implies `rv*cos(phi)>ru`, hence phi<pi/2. Insert the logarithmic-radius spherical midpoint

```text
m = sqrt(ru*rv) * (u/ru + v/rv) / |u/ru + v/rv|.
```

Both u→m and m→v are positive whenever `rv*cos(phi/2)^2>ru`, which follows strictly from the old arrow inequality. The old edge intersects this bisector ray at radius `2*ru*rv*cos(phi/2)/(ru+rv)`, strictly below `sqrt(ru*rv)`. Because the old centre is interior and the edge is exposed, the inserted point is outside the old hull: this is an architectural change, not merely triangulating an unchanged face.

That fixed-centre observation alone does **not** prove own-centred mechanics. The additional exact certificate closes this issue for ARC41_G6. If the centroid moves by d, an arrow's numerator changes from `g=x·e` to `g−d·e`. Therefore the finite sufficient condition

```text
g>0 and |d|^2 < g^2/|e|^2
```

preserves that arrow by Cauchy–Schwarz. For the 40 designed arrows in the independently rounded ARC41_G6, every arrow is a true edge, every fixed-centre g is positive, and the exact certificate proves

```text
|new_centroid − old_centroid| ∈ [19653456834,19653456835] / 10^12
min(g/|e|)                  ∈ [56617319642,56617319643] / 10^12.
```

The squared ratio is approximately 0.120497847177, strictly below 1. All 40 nonroot vertices consequently retain positive escape arrows at the **new actual filled centroid**, with root20 separately verified strictly unstable. The complete exact 40-arrow certificate is generated by CHECK_ARC_RESERVE.py, independently of previously generated reports. ## 8. Most useful continuation

Continue primarily from **PRUNE94_G6**, retaining FLEX40_G4 and FLEX78_G4 as stronger-arithmetic or larger-reserve alternatives. PRUNE94 has 34 spare edges under the requested budget; adding up to 11 exposed vertices stays within the 105-vertex sufficient bound, although that bound is not necessary and no fixed complex is imposed. The remaining 52-vertex/48-edge polar region must be addressed as a whole. The most pointed architectural target is the support fan around physical input1, whose coarse-grid splitting produces all 12 additional stable births, together with coordinated mass compensation near the current saddle interface0–2. Replacing or reorganizing that whole fan, with the exact centroid-shift/escape certificate available for suitable augmentations, is better motivated by these results than another sequence of small positive-floor steps at one death edge. The root may turn over; root20 is not a required invariant. The new finite constructions establish substantially more geometric freedom and a lower whole barrier, but they do not establish cancellation, local optimality, or impossibility of own110.
