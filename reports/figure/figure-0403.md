## 4. Topology-unrestricted Hausdorff persistence theorem

**Executed conclusion for each centered CANCEL166 G12, G8, and G7M14 body K:** the same statement holds with `d_H(K,L) <= 3/8000`.

### Proof and exact conditions

Let the filled centroid of K be zero, with `rB subset K subset RB`, where B is the Euclidean unit ball, and let `d_H(K,L)<=epsilon<r`. Support functions give

`aK subset L subset bK`, where `a=1-epsilon/r`, `b=1+epsilon/r`.

The shell `bK\aK` has zero first moment. In any unit direction its positive projected first moment is at most half its volume times Rb. Since `L\aK` is a subset of that shell and `vol(L)>=a^3 vol(K)`, the actual filled centroid g of L satisfies

`|g| <= Gamma = R*b*(b^3-a^3)/(2*a^3)`.

This is a bound on the new physical mass centroid, not an arbitrary reference point.

Suppose an old vertex x satisfies `|x|<=r_x` and

`x dot (z-x) <= -mu*|z-x|` for every z in K.

Checking every old vertex suffices: the inequality extends to their convex combinations by the triangle inequality. For y in L with `|y-x|=delta`, choose z in K with `|z-y|<=epsilon`. Expansion and the centroid bound give

`|y-g|^2 <= |x-g|^2 - 2*(mu-Gamma)*delta + delta^2 + 2*(mu+r_x)*epsilon`.

There is y0 in L with `|y0-x|<=epsilon`, and

`|y0-g|^2 >= |x-g|^2 - 2*(r_x+Gamma)*epsilon`.

Consequently the strict rational inequality

`2*(mu-Gamma)*delta - delta^2 - 2*(mu+2*r_x+Gamma)*epsilon > 0`

forces a maximum of squared radius on `L intersect closed_ball(x,delta)` to occur inside that ball, rather than on its spherical boundary. It is then a local radial maximum of L. Strict convexity of squared norm forces it to be an actual vertex. Every incident radial derivative is strictly negative: a positive derivative contradicts local maximality, and a zero derivative makes squared radius increase quadratically along that edge. Thus it is a strict unstable equilibrium. Two disjoint old-root balls force two distinct such equilibria without fixing their identities in L.
