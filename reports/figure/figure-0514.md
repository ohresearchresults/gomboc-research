### Exact arithmetic method retained

The new landscape code avoids an enormous common denominator for all polar vertices. Write raw vertices as `a_i/b_i`, actual centroid as `M/C`, and `z_i=C*a_i-b_i*M`. For a physical edge, let `e=b_i*a_j-b_j*a_i`. Its perpendicular-foot numerator and denominator are `-b_j*(z_i.e)` and `C*(e.e)`. On an interior foot, the squared chord minimum multiplied by `C^2` is

`((z_i.z_i)*(e.e)-(z_i.e)^2)/(b_i^2*(e.e))`.

Endpoint cases use the appropriate vertex norm. For a polar vertex, let `s_i=h_i*C-N_i.M`, so `q_i=C*N_i/s_i`. With `A=N_i.N_i`, `B=N_i.N_j`, `D=N_j.N_j`, the interior chord minimum divided by `C^2` is

`(A*D-B^2)/(A*s_j^2+D*s_i^2-2*B*s_i*s_j)`.

Positive common scales do not alter persistence ordering or relative depths. The code checks every weight against both endpoint heights, every positive forest arrow against increasing radius, the exact root lists against the full mechanical audits, every complete cut's maximum against its death value, and every path's minimum against the same death value. This is one exact landscape implementation with internal checks, not a claim of two independent landscape implementations.
