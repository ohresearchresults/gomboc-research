## 4. Exhaustive own-centroid mechanics

The verifier uses the following exact tests.

For a triangular face with centred vertices `p_0,p_1,p_2`, solve `G w=(1,1,1)` where `G_ij=p_i·p_j`; normalize `lambda=w/(sum w)`. The face is stable iff all three barycentric coordinates are positive.

For every directed true-edge adjacency `v→w`, define

```text
sigma(v→w)=-v·(w-v).
```

A vertex is unstable iff all its outgoing `sigma` values are positive.

For a true edge `u-v`, let `d=v-u`, `tn=-u·d`, `om=d·d-tn`, let `f=u+(tn/(d·d))d`, and let `n_1,n_2` be its two outward incident face normals. The edge is a saddle iff `tn`, `om`, and the two denominator-cleared Gram-solution numerators expressing `f` in the cone `pos(n_1,n_2)` are all positive. Display diagonals are never tested as physical edges.
