For a fixed crossing i→j and a reference-centre displacement δ,

`(v_i−g−δ)·(v_j−v_i)=(v_i−g)·(v_j−v_i)−δ·(v_j−v_i)`.

Thus a sufficient open infinity-radius preserving that negative sign is

`ρ_ij=−(v_i−g)·(v_j−v_i)/||v_j−v_i||₁`.
