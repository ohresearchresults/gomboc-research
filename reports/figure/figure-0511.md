### Exact proof and six positive bounds

For a triangle(a,b,c), set u=b−a, v=c−a and d=(u·u)(v·v)−(u·v)^2>0. The affine barycentric coordinates of the orthogonal projection of x onto its plane are

`ℓ_b(x)=((x−a)·u (v·v)−(x−a)·v (u·v))/d`,

`ℓ_c(x)=((x−a)·v (u·u)−(x−a)·u (u·v))/d`,

`ℓ_a(x)=1−ℓ_b(x)−ℓ_c(x)`.

For each of these six affine functions define

`m=min_{p_i in P} ℓ(p_i)`,

`N=V_W ℓ(g_W)+(V_P−V_W) min(0,m)`.

Affineness and uniform filled density give

`V_K ℓ(g_K)=∫_K ℓ(x) dx ≥ N`.
