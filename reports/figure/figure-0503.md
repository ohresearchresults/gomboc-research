### Proof and exact certificate recipe

Let X contain the two packet points and Y the three retained points. Construct the5x5 matrix whose first three rows are the columns `p_X0,p_X1,-p_Y0,-p_Y1,-p_Y2`, followed by rows `[1,1,0,0,0]` and `[0,0,1,1,1]`. Write

`w=A^-1(0,0,0,1,1)`, `m=min(w)`, `M=max(w)`, `B=||A^-1||_infinity`.

The exact calculation checks the inverse and `m>0`. If every coordinate of every marked point changes by at most delta, then `||DeltaA||_infinity<=5delta`. Put `eta=5delta B`. For `eta<1`, the Neumann-series inverse estimate gives

`||w'-w||_infinity <= eta M/(1-eta)`.

The chosen dyadic delta satisfies `eta(M+m)<m`, hence every perturbed weight is at least `m-eta M/(1-eta)>0`. The last two matrix rows remain unchanged, so each side still sums to1. Consequently the perturbed packet segment intersects the perturbed retained triangle. Strict linear separation is impossible.
