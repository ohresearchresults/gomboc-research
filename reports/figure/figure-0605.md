    if isinstance(x,R): return str(x)
    if isinstance(x,dict): return {str(k):encoded(v) for k,v in x.items()}

    raw=(json.dumps(encoded(value),sort_keys=True,indent=2)+chr(10)).encode()
    path.write_bytes(raw)

## Continuation priority and remaining uncertainty

The useful next input is the explicit cap at tau=1/2 or1, not another elder-detachment experiment. It has already crossed the original junior plane by a finite amount while retaining a rigorous reference-U1 construction. The first computational questions are its actual own census, its replacement stable carrier and saddle, and whether a finite correction on the rebuilt geometry can retain or restore ownU1 without reinstating an S≥2 protected-carrier restriction.

The source explicitly permits normal-fan changes and replacement of the old unstable label. It also preserves raw capped bodies even if their own equilibrium class is not211, because their actual mass response and retired original plane can be useful for continuation. Every such own-class or Q assertion still requires the unobserved exact audits.

This handoff therefore establishes a finite original-carrier replacement mechanism and a source-complete physical-mass calculation, but **does not establish cancellation of the original main Q basin or a successful physical moment-repaired endpoint**. The distinction is the principal acceptance boundary.
