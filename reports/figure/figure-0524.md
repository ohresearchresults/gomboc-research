## 3. Completed theorem: a five-level material gate with the actual filled centroid

Let K denote the raw HPRUNE57 body, g its own volume centroid, and let its five exact positive P lifetimes be

`B_i=birth_i−death_i>0`.

These are **absolute squared-radius lifetimes**, not the relative depths in the preceding display. Put

```text
R2 = max_{v vertex of K} |v−g|²
T  = a rational upper bound for max_{v vertex of K}|v|
G  = a rational upper bound for |g|, with G<=T
r  = a positive rational lower bound for the inradius of K−g
L1 = max_{v vertex of K} (|v_x|+|v_y|+|v_z|).
```

The program obtains T,G by exact upward square-root rounding on the grid10^-9, and r by exact downward square-root rounding on the same grid. All rounding decisions are integer comparisons.

For each lifetime B_i define the explicit positive rational number

```text
alpha_i = B_i*r/(256*R2*T)
a_i =1/(1+alpha_i)
b_i =1/(1−alpha_i).
```

**Material-envelope theorem.** For every full-dimensional convex polyhedron W satisfying

`a_i K ⊆ W ⊆ b_i K`,

uniformly filled and evaluated at its own centroid, every parent bar whose lifetime is at least B_i has a distinct surviving positive P bar. In particular, sorting the five lifetimes in decreasing order gives five closed material envelopes certifying respectively at least two, three, four, five, and six strict unstable equilibria. This remains true with arbitrary new vertices/facets, changed maximal complexes, root turnover, and additional weak equilibria elsewhere. No E restriction is needed for the theorem; hence it applies in particular to every such W with E<=249.

For the smallest lifetime, **all five bars survive**. This is not a quota-six objective and not an omission of the senior basin.

### Actual-mass proof

Write alpha=alpha_i, a=1/(1+alpha), b=1/(1−alpha). Decompose the genuinely filled W into aK and its added material. Since

```text
a³ Vol(K) <= Vol(W) <= b³ Vol(K),
```

the fraction of W outside aK is at most

`theta=1−(a/b)^3`.

The centroid of that material lies in bK. Therefore, without prescribing a free centre,

```text
|g_W−g| <= (1−a)G +theta*(bT+aG).
```

Also `d_H(K,W)<=(b−1)T`. After both bodies are centred at their actual filled centroids,

```text
d_H(K−g,W−g_W) <= E(alpha)
E(alpha)=(b−1)T+(1−a)G+theta*(bT+aG).
```

This argument permits both removal from K and addition outside K: aK, not K, is the fixed retained material.

Using G<=T and theta<=6alpha gives

```text
E(alpha) <= [14alpha/(1−alpha²)]T <16alpha T
```

for alpha<=1/4. Here `B_i<=R2` and `r<=sqrt(R2)<=2T`, so the specified alpha_i is at most1/128. Consequently

```text
E(alpha_i)/r <= B_i/(16R2) <=1/16.
```

The centred radial squared functions consequently differ by at most

```text

```

The radial-bar stability principle then gives the asserted distinct surviving bars. Their matched lifetimes are at least `B_j−2Delta_i`; for B_j>=B_i this is at least `95B_j/128>0`. The same-centre, true-skeleton/complete-chord interpretation and radial stability conventions are those already accepted in the journal. 

For a convex polyhedron, a vertex with a zero outgoing radial derivative is not a radial local maximum: along that edge the second-order squared-radius term is positive. ## 4. Exact finite mass control by four real pyramids

This construction is useful precisely because the S1 reserve is small: it avoids treating a later centroid drift as an independent repair problem.

Choose four maximal facets f_i. Let their outward equations be `n_i·x=h_i`, their area centroids be c_i, and define the positive rational coefficient

```text
beta_i = area(f_i)/(3|n_i|).
```

No irrational arithmetic is needed: for an oriented triangulation of the facet,

`beta_i=Σ n_i·((b−a)×(c−a))/(6 n_i·n_i)`.

Suppose positive rational weights lambda_i satisfy

```text
Σlambda_i=1,
Σlambda_i c_i=g.
```

Choose rational directions u_i satisfying the seven linear equations

```text
n_i·u_i=lambda_i/beta_i       for i=0,1,2,3,
Σlambda_i u_i=0.             three scalar equations
```

These equations have rank7 whenever the four normals are not all parallel. There are therefore five free rational parameters. A row dependence would require a common vector simultaneously parallel to every n_i; two nonparallel normals force that vector and all row coefficients to vanish.

For positive rational epsilon set

`p_i(epsilon)=c_i+epsilon*u_i`.

As long as each new apex realizes its own facet pyramid and the full union is convex, its added volume is exactly `epsilon*lambda_i`. Its added first moment is

`epsilon*lambda_i*(3c_i+p_i)/4`.

Summing gives, **for every admissible epsilon, not just to first order**,

```text
Vol(K_new)=Vol(K)+epsilon,
M(K_new)=M(K)+epsilon*g,
centroid(K_new)=g.
```

The actual matter is K together with four ordinary uniform pyramids. There is no density ballast and no free-centre substitution.

If the four facets have k_i sides and the independent-pyramid complex is valid, the exact counts are

```text

E_new=165+Σk_i,
F_new=53+Σk_i.
```

Thus `Σk_i<=84` is the exact present edge-budget condition. The source searches for a positive four-facet carrier satisfying this condition; it prefers facets avoiding all six old U, but does not assume that such a preferred carrier exists.

### Coupling a complete finite surgery to the reservoirs

Fix positive pyramid volumes tau_i and retain their four base facets unchanged. Suppose another surgery changes the base body's volume and first moment by DeltaV,DeltaM. Tangential apex corrections d_i satisfying

```text
n_i·d_i=0,
Σtau_i d_i=−4(DeltaM−g*DeltaV)
```

restore **exactly** the same own centroid g. This is again a rank7 rational linear system with five free parameters. The volume and first moment of the other surgery must be the actual finite ones. A first-order approximation is not substituted.

For rational cut geometry the correction is rational. If a cut has polynomial volume/first moment along a fixed incidence interval, the apex correction is polynomial divided by the fixed rational tau_i. This makes full-interval side and mechanical guards available without an iterative mass-closure step.

The required conditions are substantive: the four base polygons must remain unchanged; the pyramids must have disjoint interiors outside the base body; every proposed maximal face must support all material; and S/U/H plus both complete landscapes must be rechecked. Since the construction keeps the actual centroid exactly g, **all six old strict U survive throughout that entire cell**.
