## 3. Whole-root proof

### 3.1 Exhaustive escape-tree reduction, including weak signs

Write `x_i=p_i-g` and `A=X G X^T`, where the rows of X are the x_i. Thus A is one actual nonzero PSD Gram matrix. The volume centroid is strictly interior: averaging each facet's positive interior slack gives positive centroid slack. Hence every vertex differs from the centroid, and each root or child reference norm used below is nonzero.

A vertex i is strict unstable exactly when

\[
x_i^{\mathsf T}G(x_j-x_i)<0
\]

for every true neighbour j. If it is not strict unstable, at least one adjacent vertex satisfies the weak reverse inequality. Choosing one such neighbour gives

\[
s_i:=A_{ii}-A_{i,p(i)}\le0.
\tag{1}
\]

Moreover,

\[
\|x_{p(i)}\|_G^2-\|x_i\|_G^2
=2x_i^{\mathsf T}G(x_{p(i)}-x_i)+\|x_{p(i)}-x_i\|_G^2>0.
\]

Therefore no chosen arrows cycle. If b were the only strict unstable vertex, the choices would form a spanning tree directed to normal root8. This adjacent-shadow reduction is the standard one; its application here is reproduced explicitly rather than taken as an unverified graph surrogate.
