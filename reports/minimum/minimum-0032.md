## 3. New exact geometric lemma: the terminal triangle is strictly obtuse and cannot be stable

This section is a complete symbolic argument. It does not depend on floating-point ordering, numerical optimization, a catalogue alias, or an unreturned certificate.

Let `M` be SPD and write

`⟨a,b⟩ = aᵀMb`, `D(i,j) = ⟨x_i,x_j−x_i⟩`.

### Local lemma

For three distinct points `x5,x6,x7`, suppose

`D(6,5) ≥ 0`, `D(7,6) ≥ 0`, and `D(7,5) < 0`.

Then the angle at `x6` in triangle 567 is strictly obtuse in metric `M`:

`⟨x5−x6, x7−x6⟩ < 0`.

The complete identity is

`⟨x5−x6, x7−x6⟩ = D(7,5) − D(6,5) − D(7,6)`.

The right side is strictly negative under the stated hypotheses. Equivalently,

`‖x5−x7‖²_M > ‖x5−x6‖²_M + ‖x6−x7‖²_M`.

No genericity or strictness of the two chosen escape inequalities is needed.

There is also a stronger projection statement. Suppose the three points form a nondegenerate facet triangle, the reference point is the origin, and `q` is the metric-orthogonal projection of the origin onto the facet plane. Put `y_i=x_i−q` for `i∈{5,6,7}`. Metric orthogonality gives

`D(i,j)=⟨y_i,y_j−y_i⟩`

for these three vertices. Since `D(7,5)<0`, `y7` cannot be zero. Consequently,

`⟨y6,y7⟩ ≥ ‖y7‖²_M > 0`,

and therefore `y6≠0`. The other escape inequality gives

`⟨y6,y5⟩ ≥ ‖y6‖²_M > 0`.

Thus the linear functional `y↦⟨y6,y⟩` is strictly positive on all three vertices `y5,y6,y7`, and hence throughout their convex hull. The origin is not in that convex hull. Therefore `q` lies strictly outside the closed facet triangle. In particular, this triangle is not a stable facet, even under a convention allowing a boundary projection.

### Why a certified surviving broom would satisfy the third inequality

Assume the upstream 285-tree exclusion and omitted-cell implication have been accepted. A hypothetical mono-unstable nine-vertex body then has the surviving broom `T`, with actual terminal triangular maximal facet 567.

The first two local hypotheses are chosen weak escape inequalities of `T`. Suppose, contrary to the third hypothesis, that `D(7,5)≥0`. Because 567 is an actual triangular maximal facet, **57 is an actual true edge**. Replace only the parent of vertex 7, obtaining

`T' = (0,0,1,2,3,4,5,5,0)`.

This remains a weak true-edge rooted spanning tree. It is not isomorphic to `T`: `T` has just one vertex with two children, namely its root, whereas `T'` has two such vertices, the root and vertex 5. Thus `T'` belongs to the other 285 rooted shapes and is excluded by the upstream certificates. Hence `D(7,5)<0`.

This argument uses an actual facet edge, not an inserted potential nonedge.
