# External research consulted

- Tancredi Schettini Gherardini, *An explicit mono-monostatic polyhedron*, arXiv:2609.07827, 2026-09-07, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07827 . Primary abstract reports a smallest construction with 56,946 facets, far above the target. Author claim, not replayed here.
- Domokos, Kovacs, Langi, Regos, Varga, *Balancing polyhedra*, arXiv:1810.05382, https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.05382 . Research background and equilibrium-class complexity; its mechanical complexity convention differs from the owner's C=V+E+F.
- Domokos and Kovacs, *Conway's Spiral and a Discrete Gomboc with 21 Point Masses*, https://doi.org/10.1080/00029890.2023.2241336 . Equal vertex point masses are not uniform solid mass; abstract explicitly reports obstruction to the similar homogeneous construction. Not a qualifying source body.
- Varkonyi and Domokos, *Static equilibria of rigid bodies: dice, pebbles and the Poincare-Hopf theorem*, DOI 10.1007/s00332-005-0691-8, https://gomboc.eu/docs/93.pdf . Search located explicit smooth two-parameter surface construction; not yet extracted or used as data.
