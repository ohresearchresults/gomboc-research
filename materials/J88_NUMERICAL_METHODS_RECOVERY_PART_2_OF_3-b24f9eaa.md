## Why these methods differ
rootchart88 keeps all old nonroots excluded and optimizes only the unwanted root's outgoing score; it does not eliminate the whole basin. In three steps from exactG8 with witnessfloor1e-5/trust.005, its deficit decreased .13463240443027538 -> .12472803386266691 while P worsened to .45640852598855597.
S1Model removes ALL primal nonroot constraints, retaining the actual unique stable polar root, exclusion of other stable roots, the elder physical root, and a strict convex fan. Temporary primal pairs and carrier migrations are allowed and recorded. The objective uses all current positive numerical P bars (the imported float helper has depth cutoff1e-14, so an exact endpoint check is still mandatory). It cannot legitimately claim no weak roots.
BundleModel handles near-tied birth vertices by building the current high component and a complete widest route to the elder. Its epigraph includes every component birth radius and every chosen route edge death radius. This is a specified finite surrogate, not a global certificate that every evolving vertex stays on the same component.
TreeModel uses a maximin directed global-chord arborescence to the elder. Positive exact scores on this tree would imply every nonroot has a positive outgoing chord, hence U1; negative intermediate scores permit root changes. Its bottleneck is distinct from the deepest P bar. On exactG8 the bottleneck is -.4564692091720477 at arc33->27, whereas the P-improved BUNDLE_A nominee has bottleneck -.5018215110217304. Four TREE_A steps raised it to -.4450528253703354; resulting numerical S1,U17,primaryP .44532114658589184, with additional positive bars. No110 is claimed.
