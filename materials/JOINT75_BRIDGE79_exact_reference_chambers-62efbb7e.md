## Proof and algorithm semantics
For a fixed polyhedron, vertex normal-cone predicates and facet interior-foot predicates are affine inequalities in the reference point. Their critical regions are closed convex polyhedra. All polytopes in the cover are clipped with exact Fraction arithmetic, with an exact planar cap hull, and remain full dimensional; lower-dimensional cut results may be discarded because a strict110 with no additional weak equilibria has an open reference chamber. A forbidden critical cone covering every cell vertex covers the entire cell. Otherwise its complement is covered by closed outside halfspaces, whose overlaps do not compromise completeness. The quota solver allows one S carrier: when two S cones contain the entire cell it is impossible; when the centre has two S, every target must escape at least one, giving the union of their outside halfspaces. Other U cones are forbidden because U6 is explicitly retained. A node cap reports UNKNOWN, never an exclusion. The positive searches stop at node4, leaving unvisited cells; only existence, not global enumeration, follows. The source is specialised to the literal simplicial input hulls, and all exact mechanical claims use maximal facets/true edges from exhaustive supporting-triple enumeration in the dependency.
